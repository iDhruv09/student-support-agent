# Student Policy Handbook - PGDM Programme (Batch 2025-27)

## Fee Payment Deadline and Late Fee
**Summary:** Trimester fees must be paid on or before the due date; a late fee of Rs 100 per day applies after the due date.
**Rules:**
- The total tuition fee for each trimester and its due date are shown in the student account.
- Fees must be paid in full on or before the due date unless an installment plan has been approved.
- A late fee of Rs 100 per calendar day is charged for every day after the due date until the fee is fully paid.
- The late fee is calculated on the number of days delayed, not on the amount outstanding.
- If fees remain unpaid for more than 30 days after the due date, the student may be barred from the end-term examinations of that trimester.
**Exceptions:** Late fee is waived only in the case of a verified payment portal failure (see Payment Portal Failure) or with written approval from the Accounts Office.
**Contact:** Accounts Office - accounts@institute.edu
**Keywords:** fee due date, late fee, fine, penalty, pay late, overdue fees, unpaid fees, deadline, last date to pay

## Payment Portal Failure
**Summary:** If the online payment portal fails on the due date, the student may pay on the next working day without a late fee, provided they report the failure on the same day.
**Rules:**
- A portal failure means the payment website is down, shows an error, or the transaction fails or is declined due to a system problem.
- The student must email accounts@institute.edu on the same day as the failure.
- The email must include the student ID, a screenshot of the error, and the failed transaction reference number if available.
- If this email is sent on the same day, the student may pay on the next working day with no late fee.
- Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding institute holidays. If the failure happens on a Friday, the next working day is Monday.
- If the student does not report the failure on the same day, the normal late fee of Rs 100 per day applies.
- If money was deducted from the bank account but the payment does not show in the student account, the student should raise a support ticket; the amount is usually auto-refunded by the bank within 5-7 working days.
**Contact:** Accounts Office - accounts@institute.edu
**Keywords:** payment portal failed, payment failed, transaction failed, website down, error while paying, pay next working day, money deducted, payment not reflected

## Fee Installments
**Summary:** The trimester fee may be paid in two installments only with prior written approval from the Accounts Office.
**Rules:**
- Students may request to split the trimester fee into two equal installments.
- The request must be submitted to the Accounts Office at least 7 days before the fee due date.
- The first installment (50%) is due on the original due date; the second installment (50%) is due within 30 days after that.
- Installment plans are not automatically approved; the student must receive written approval.
- If an installment is missed, the late fee of Rs 100 per day applies from the installment due date.
**Contact:** Accounts Office - accounts@institute.edu
**Keywords:** installment, pay in parts, split payment, EMI, partial payment, second installment

## Fee Refund
**Summary:** Fees are refundable only if the student withdraws before the trimester starts, minus a Rs 5,000 processing charge.
**Rules:**
- A student who withdraws from the programme before the start of a trimester gets a refund of that trimester's fee minus Rs 5,000 processing charge.
- No refund is given for withdrawal after the trimester has started.
- Refund requests must be submitted in writing to the Accounts Office with a cancelled cheque or bank details.
- Refunds are processed within 30 working days of approval.
- Excess or duplicate payments are fully refunded without any processing charge.
**Contact:** Accounts Office - accounts@institute.edu
**Keywords:** refund, money back, withdraw, withdrawal, cancel admission, duplicate payment, excess payment

## Merit Scholarship
**Summary:** Students with CGPA 8.0 or above get a 20% tuition fee waiver, reviewed every trimester.
**Rules:**
- Eligibility: CGPA of 8.0 or above at the end of the previous trimester and attendance of at least 75% in every course.
- Benefit: a 20% waiver on the tuition fee of the next trimester.
- Merit scholarships are awarded automatically to eligible students; no separate application is needed.
- Renewal is reviewed at the end of every trimester.
- The scholarship is withdrawn if CGPA falls below 8.0 or attendance falls below 75% in any course.
- A withdrawn scholarship can be awarded again in a later trimester if the student meets the criteria again.
- A student cannot hold a merit scholarship and a need-based scholarship at the same time; the higher benefit is applied.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** merit scholarship, CGPA, eligibility, 20% waiver, fee discount, scholarship withdrawn, renewal

## Need-Based Scholarship
**Summary:** Students with annual family income below Rs 6,00,000 can apply for a need-based scholarship within the first 15 days of the trimester.
**Rules:**
- Eligibility: annual family income below Rs 6,00,000 and attendance of at least 75%.
- Benefit: a waiver of up to 30% of the tuition fee, decided by the Scholarship Committee.
- The application must be submitted to the Program Office within the first 15 days of the trimester.
- Required documents: income certificate or parents' income tax return, and a filled application form.
- Applications without income proof are rejected.
- Decisions are communicated within 21 working days of the application deadline.
- A rejected student may reapply in the next trimester with complete documents.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** need-based scholarship, financial aid, family income, income proof, apply for scholarship, scholarship rejected, scholarship pending

## Scholarship Status and Appeals
**Summary:** Students can check their scholarship status through the support agent and may appeal a decision within 10 working days.
**Rules:**
- Possible statuses are: Approved, Under review, Application pending, Rejected, Withdrawn, and Not applied.
- "Under review" or "Application pending" means a decision has not yet been made.
- A student may appeal a rejection or withdrawal by writing to the Program Office within 10 working days of the decision, with supporting documents.
- The appeal decision is final.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** scholarship status, appeal, scholarship rejected, under review, pending scholarship, reconsider

## Elective and Course Change
**Summary:** Electives can be changed only within the first 7 days of a trimester through the Program Office.
**Rules:**
- A student may change an elective course within the first 7 calendar days of a trimester.
- The request must be submitted to the Program Office and is subject to seat availability.
- Only one elective change is allowed per trimester.
- Changes after 7 days are not permitted, except for medical reasons approved by the Program Director.
- Core (compulsory) courses cannot be changed or dropped.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** change elective, course change, drop course, switch subject, add course, elective deadline

## Programme Change
**Summary:** Switching between PGDM specialisations (AI&DS and BDA) is allowed only before the start of Trimester 2 and needs Program Office approval.
**Rules:**
- A student may request to move between PGDM AI&DS and PGDM BDA only before the start of Trimester 2.
- The request needs approval from the Program Office and depends on seat availability.
- Any fee difference must be paid before the change is applied.
- After Trimester 2 begins, programme changes are not permitted.
- Approved changes are updated in the student record only after verification.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** change programme, switch specialisation, AI&DS to BDA, BDA to AI&DS, transfer programme

## Updating Personal Details
**Summary:** Changes to phone number or programme in the official record require verification and approval and are applied only after approval.
**Rules:**
- Students can request changes to their registered phone number or programme.
- Every change request is verified and approved or rejected by the Program Office.
- A change is not applied until it is approved.
- If a request is rejected, the existing record remains unchanged.
- Students can only request changes to their own record, never to another student's record.
- Phone numbers must be valid 10-digit Indian mobile numbers.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** update phone number, change mobile number, update details, change record, personal information, correction

## Student Data Privacy
**Summary:** Students may access only their own records; sharing or requesting another student's data is not allowed.
**Rules:**
- Fee, scholarship, CGPA and personal details are private to each student.
- A student can view only their own records after logging in.
- Staff will never ask for OTPs, passwords or PINs. Students must never share them, including in chat.
- Any OTP or password shared by mistake is not stored by the institute system.
- Suspected misuse of student data must be reported to the Program Office immediately.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** privacy, my data, another student's data, OTP, password, data protection, security

## Attendance
**Summary:** A minimum of 75% attendance in each course is required to sit the end-term examination.
**Rules:**
- Attendance is calculated separately for each course.
- Students with less than 75% attendance in a course are not allowed to appear for that course's end-term examination.
- Medical leave with a valid certificate submitted within 3 working days may be considered by the Program Director.
- Attendance below 75% also leads to withdrawal of a merit scholarship.
**Contact:** Program Office - programoffice@institute.edu
**Keywords:** attendance, 75 percent, shortage, medical leave, debarred, eligible for exam

## End-Term Examinations
**Summary:** Students need 75% attendance and no fee dues older than 30 days to appear for end-term exams.
**Rules:**
- End-term examinations are held at the end of every trimester.
- To be eligible, a student must have at least 75% attendance in the course and no fee dues outstanding for more than 30 days.
- Students who miss an exam due to a medical emergency may apply for a make-up exam within 5 working days with a medical certificate.
- Results are published within 21 working days after the examinations end.
**Contact:** Examination Cell - exams@institute.edu
**Keywords:** exam, end-term, eligibility, make-up exam, missed exam, results, hall ticket

## Support Requests and Escalation
**Summary:** Students can raise a support ticket for academic, fee or technical issues; the team responds within 2 working days.
**Rules:**
- A support ticket can be raised for academic, fee, scholarship, record or technical issues.
- Each ticket gets a unique ticket ID that the student should keep for follow-up.
- The support team responds within 2 working days.
- If there is no response in 2 working days, or the issue is urgent, the student may escalate to the Program Office quoting the ticket ID.
- Questions not covered by this handbook should be raised as a support ticket.
**Contact:** Support Desk - support@institute.edu
**Keywords:** support ticket, raise complaint, help, issue, problem, escalate, no response, contact
